# Campus Customs: Design (Problem 10)

**Concept: "Augusta, stardate 2026."** It takes the calm, premium feel of the Masters (Augusta green, Masters yellow, azalea pink, cream paper, classic serif type) and adds a *Star Trek*-style interface on top: LCARS pill buttons, holo glows, a moving grid "fairway" and a scan-line. The design lives in `frontend/src/theme.css`, which loads after `index.css`, re-maps the colour tokens and re-skins every component without changing any layout.

| Area | What changed | Why it helps customers stay and buy |
|---|---|---|
| **Colour** | Augusta green `#006747` / deep `#012a1c`, gold `#f2c75c`, azalea `#e84a7f`, cream `#f7f4ea` background. Azalea is kept for **buy actions** (Add to cart, cart badge, prices on the Leaderboard). | The palette signals "premium, heritage" rather than "bargain bin", which supports $58–$98 prices. Giving buy actions one accent colour that appears nowhere else makes them stand out. |
| **Type** | **Playfair Display** (serif, Masters-style) for headlines and product names. **Orbitron** (futuristic) for labels, nav, prices and buttons. **Inter** for body text. | This gives a clear hierarchy: the serif says what the product is, Orbitron says what to do or what it costs, and Inter is for reading. Shoppers scan faster and find the price and buttons immediately. |
| **Nav** | A dark-green glass "bridge console" with a gold hairline, a serif brand with a gold italic "Customs" and an "EST. NEW HAVEN" tag. The active page is a gold pill. | People always know where they are, and the brand feels like an established store. |
| **Hero** | Headline "Bulldog tradition, *tailored for tomorrow.*" over an animated perspective-grid fairway, with azalea and gold glow orbs. Gold "Shop the collection" pill. | A striking first screen makes people stay past the first few seconds, and there is a single obvious next step. |
| **Leaderboard (Home)** | Top picks shown as a Masters-style **scoreboard**: rank, thumbnail, name, live stock and an azalea price tag, each row linking to the product. | It's a golf touch shoppers will recognise, and showing real stock next to each price quietly adds urgency. |
| **Product cards** | Cream-to-white "studio" backdrop behind each photo, a serif name, an Orbitron price, and a gold edge glow and lift on hover. Low-stock badges are in azalea. | Photos look like merchandise rather than raw uploads. The hover response invites clicks, and scarcity is visible before the click. |
| **Product page** | The image is framed like a trophy case with gold "viewfinder" corners. Pill-shaped size buttons (the chosen one glows gold) and an azalea **Add to cart · $98.00** button. | This is where people buy, so it gets the most finish, and the price shown on the button removes any doubt before clicking. |
| **Showcase (chat results)** | A green holo panel with a slow scan-line sweep and a gold match count. | Results arriving from the assistant feel like an event, so shoppers look at them and keep browsing. |
| **Chat** | Styled as a "comms panel": a gold-bordered pulsing hail button, an Orbitron header with a green "online" dot, gold-edged assistant bubbles and green user bubbles. The "👀 Viewing" line is in gold. | A distinctive, alive-looking assistant gets opened more, and conversations lead to cart adds (Problem 9). |
| **Motion** | Pages fade in on route change. Hover lifts, the fairway grid drifts, the showcase scans, the chat button pulses. All motion is turned off for `prefers-reduced-motion`. | Subtle movement makes the site feel polished and responsive without being distracting, and it stays accessible. |
| **Cart & footer** | The cart drawer has a green header and gold edge. The footer is a green "clubhouse" sign-off with a gold rule. | Checkout surfaces look as trustworthy as the rest of the site, and the brand frames every page. |

**Deliberately not done:** I didn't use Augusta's or the Masters' logos, or the trademarked "A tradition unlike any other" slogan; the colour and style are only inspired by them. I didn't add any invented promos or shipping promises. The layout and every Problem 3–9 feature are unchanged (the theme only reskins).
